# Changelog

Newest first. The app shows the newest entry once after an update (Settings → What's new). Keep this file, `src/lib/changelog.js` and the version in `manifest.json` in step; a test checks.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-05)

- Inbox tab: your Canvas messages in their own tab, with Unread, Starred, Sent and Archived views and a filter by class.
- Message your instructors, TAs and classmates without opening Canvas: write a new message (press C) or reply right in the conversation. Messages go through Canvas.
- Star, archive and mark messages unread; Canvas shows the same.
- Canvas groups: your project teams and study groups now have their own pages (Courses → Your groups) with announcements, discussions, pages, files and members.
- Classes that post assignments the week they’re due no longer show as At risk: points not posted yet count as work to come, not zeros.
- No syllabus for a class? Its syllabus window now lets you type in exams, deadlines, class times and grading (including a class total, like 1000 points).

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-05)

- Themes tab (palette icon, top right): pick Neko the cat, Kero the frog, Nami the shark, Yumi the polar bear or Hachi the shiba, each with its own colors.
- Our cat mascot is now called Neko.
- Your own events: click New event on the Calendar (or an empty time slot) for pre-class readings, study sessions and more. They can repeat weekly.
- Adding syllabi is now front and center: Home, the Calendar and Settings show which classes still need their class times.
- No class times in your syllabus? Type them in (Lecture, Quiz section, Lab…) and they show as blocks every week.
- Fixed a background error that could appear on chrome://extensions after reloading.
- Long details in compact lists now shorten neatly instead of overlapping.
- The search bar is gone from the top bar, for a cleaner look.
- Make Neko yours: six quick, skippable questions (study buddy, your name, GPA goal, workload, reminders, look) in setup and in Settings.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-20)

- Works with any school's Canvas: choose yours in setup (Settings → General → School).
- Letter grades (A, A−, B+…) or a 4.0 decimal scale, and semesters as well as quarters.
- Import a syllabus (PDF, Word or text) on a class page → Syllabus dates. You check every date before it's saved.
- Try it with sample data: a demo mode with a made-up student.
- Settings → About: disclaimers and a way to report problems.
- Public build: read-only (no submitting or posting), so it asks Chrome for fewer permissions.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-07)

- First version: due dates, grades and GPA, calendar, course content, reminders and Neko (then called Mochi).
